Hi, welcome to my homepage. I am currently a PhD candidate in the [Artificial Intelligence Laboratory](https://nkdailab.github.io/) in the [Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering](https://eee.sustech.edu.cn/) from [Southern University of Science and Technology (南方科技大学)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/). I am a self-driven, quick starter, passionate researcher with a curious mind who enjoys solving complex and challenging real-world computer vision problems.

I received the B.Eng. degree in mechanical design manufacture and automation (机械设计制作及其自动化), and the M.Sc. degree in electronic science and technology (电子科学与技术) from [Xidian University (西安电子科技大学)](https://www.xidian.edu.cn/), Xi'an, China, in 2019, and 2023.

My research interests include machine learning, computer vision, object detection, multimodality, and controllable text-to-image generation.

Thanks for the nice open-source [academic homepage template](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io).
